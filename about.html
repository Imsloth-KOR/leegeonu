ABOUT

A name is given, not made.

Geonu Lee is the name I was given. But a name alone is not enough to describe who I am.

I am interested in time, possible futures, artificial intelligence, nature, and the tools that allow digital forms to become analogue presence.

DnA is a frame for observing the space between digital possibility and analogue presence. It begins with time, expands through artificial intelligence, returns to nature, and seeks physical form through tools of fabrication.

DnA — Digital and Analogue A quiet archive for possible futures.
